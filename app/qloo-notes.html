SEO and content audit

Qloo.com · October 21, 2026 · A courtesy contribution from a hackathon participant

No critical technical SEO blocker confirmed

The 15 unique marketing pages sampled returned 200 with readable initial HTML, metadata and canonicals. All six stories already have Article and breadcrumb markup. The actionable findings below are limited to two content corrections and two SEO/navigation opportunities.

  1. Content correction

    Scope the customer outcome accurately

    The Match Group headline is broader than the body's Yuzu cohort and Likes metric.
    Match Group story

    Use the same customer-approved cohort and metric in the headline, cards, announcement and demo copy. This is an accuracy correction; ranking impact is unmeasured.

  2. Content correction

    Mark the completed event as past

    The July–August 2025 hackathon still had a live label on the review date.
    Events

    Correct the archive label and calculate event status from start/end dates and timezone. The 2026 event is separate and current on October 21.

  3. SEO opportunity

    Review historical URL destinations

    Four sampled articles redirect to Resources; two industry pages redirect to Home. All eight checked redirects work in one hop.
    Example historical URL

    Use relevant retained content and landing-page data to choose better destinations where warranted. A broad hub may be appropriate; no traffic loss or penalty was established.

  4. SEO opportunity

    Link capabilities to customer proof

    Capability sections provide substantial explanations, but their main-content links lead to anchors and demos rather than customer stories.
    Capabilities

    Add relevant links to existing customer evidence and link back to the capability. Give developers a verified implementation route where supported.

Supporting assessment

What already works

All 15 sampled pages have a title, description, canonical URL and one H1. Core copy and links are present in initial HTML. All 15 JSON-LD blocks parsed; Organization, WebSite, Article and breadcrumb foundations already exist. The marketing robots policy permits public crawling and the sitemap lists 18 entries.

Marketing robots · Sitemap

Optional observations, not confirmed main-site defects

Visible publication dates could help contextualize the six customer stories; dates and Qloo authorship already exist in their markup. Customer-approved methodology notes could make numerical outcomes easier to evaluate.

The separate documentation host disallows wildcard crawling. Its intent is unknown, so this is an owner-policy question rather than a confirmed marketing-site defect. Documentation bodies were not crawled. Missing llms.txt is not an SEO defect or a prerequisite for Google's generative search features.

Documentation robots · Google guidance

How to verify the suggested changes

Confirm the result wording with its measurement owner and check every occurrence. Test event states before, during and after the event window. Keep relevant historical redirects in one hop. Verify that contextual links reach working pages that actually demonstrate the capability.

Google's relevant-redirect guidance

Scope and limits

Core sample: 15 unique canonical marketing pages including six customer stories. Eight historical URLs were checked separately. Legal-page bodies, documentation bodies and authenticated product behavior were outside the content review.

Rankings, traffic, conversions, actual search-assistant citations, field Core Web Vitals and real-device behavior were not measured. No numerical score or projected improvement is assigned. No website changes have been implemented.
