## Scores (four criteria, 1-5 each, one sentence of evidence per score)

**Intelligent and thoughtful use of LLMs + the Qloo API: 4.** The live Bad Bunny answer named the model, a 0.3s `artist_places` call, and five Qloo ids, and "Ask again without Qloo" removed the venues and refused to invent them, but the main "where should I open" path is a labeled non-model template and the Culebra model answer restated the island rate with the wrong unit.

**Technical implementation and execution: 3.** The scripted bakery-to-San-Juan-to-restaurants path returned sourced counts in about two seconds and `/compare` rendered a full six-pair transcript, but the same session showed 941 vs Food 50, rank 76 vs 78, two closing times for La Placita, a phone submit that vanished, and a Culebra answer whose map was still Adjuntas.

**Originality and creativity: 4.** Pairing a Qloo heatmap with Census establishment counts for every municipio, then letting an artist query land on real places with a one-click ablation, is a specific product and not a chat box on a map; the frozen October 14 compare page and the English leftovers in the Spanish UI keep it short of a 5.

**Real-world application and potential impact: 3.** A Spanish-speaking owner can get a sourced Adjuntas card that says five bakeries, $19,549 median income, and "not proof of demand," and the app refused to invent rent, an owner, or an API key, but the quadrant title "muchos negocios," the 10× rate slip, stale-or-conflicting hours, and a map that did not follow the question are enough to send her to the wrong town.

## What a rival entry would need to beat this

A rival has to beat a working ablation, not a slide about one. This entry already shows, on a live button and on a stamped six-question page, that removing Qloo tools turns named venues into a refusal. A rival that only wraps Qloo in a nicer chat will lose that test.

To win, a rival would need all of the following, visible in the demo: the raw tool payload next to the sentence, including the rate and its unit; the map and the answer locked to the same town; hours either checked live or labeled with the catalog date in the sentence itself; and a Spanish first screen whose recommendation a non-technical owner can act on Monday, with the absolute count and the rent gap stated in one place. Beating the prose is not enough. The with-Qloo and without-Qloo answers for the same question have to be re-run in the room, on a question the judge types, and the numbers have to survive a pocket calculator.
